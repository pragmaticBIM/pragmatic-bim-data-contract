# Abstract door connector products

Source: [`door-connector-products.skos.ttl`](sources/door-connector-product.ttl)

## Scheme

- **definition (de):** Produkttyp-Klassifikation fuer Tuer-Verbindungen (ConnectionPhysical mit funktionalem Typ access; IFC-physischer Typ door). Fuer Katalog-, Spezifikations- und Kostenworkflows; mit abstrakter Materialklassifikation fuer Rahmen- und Fuellsubstanz kombinieren.
- **definition (en):** Product-type classification for door connectors (ConnectionPhysical with functional type access; IFC physical type door). For catalog, specification, and cost workflows; pair with abstract material classification for frame and infill substance.
- **prefLabel (de):** Abstrakte Tuer-Verbindungsprodukte
- **prefLabel (en):** Abstract door connector products
- **title (en):** Abstract door connector products

Panels, tracks, springs, and drive are prefabricated, assembled in the opening, anchored to the structural reveal or lintel, and commissioned together with drive, control, and safety devices. Suitable for garages, loading bays, and industrial halls with frequent operating cycles; requires edge and crush protection, a defined opening speed, manual emergency operation, and periodic inspection of the moving parts.</td>

<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="description">Türkonstruktion, deren Blatt überwiegend aus Glas besteht, entweder rahmenlos mit Klemmbeschlägen oder in schmalem Metallrahmen, als Dreh-, Schiebe- oder Automatikflügel in einer verglasten Trennwand. Die Scheiben werden werkseitig vorgespannt oder als Verbundsicherheitsglas hergestellt, Bodentürschliesser oder Laufschiene und die umgebende Verglasung werden vor Ort gesetzt und ausgerichtet, danach wird das Blatt eingestellt und die Bedienung in Betrieb genommen. Geeignet für Eingangshallen und innere Öffnungen mit durchgehendem Sichtbezug; die Bedienung muss für alle Nutzenden sicher bleiben und das Glas gefahrloses Durchgehen sowie eine erkennbare Markierung gewährleisten.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="scope_note"></td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="label">Glazed door system</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="definition">Door predominantly glazed with minimal frame, including automatic sliding glass doors and full-height glazed entrances.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="description">Door assembly whose leaf consists mainly of glass, either frameless with clamped fittings or in a narrow metal frame, operating as a hinged, sliding, or automatic leaf within a glazed screen. Panes are toughened or laminated in the factory, the floor spring or guide rail and the surrounding screen are set and levelled on site, and the leaf is aligned and its operation commissioned. Suitable for entrance halls and interior openings with visual continuity; operation must stay safe for all users and the glass has to provide safe passage and be clearly marked.</td>

Leaf and frame are welded, coated, and pre-fitted in the workshop, the frame is anchored into the reveal and the leaf hung with heavy-duty hinges, locking, and where required closers and panic hardware. Suitable for technical rooms, escape routes, and exterior or heavily used openings with fire compartmentation, smoke control, or burglar resistance duty.</td>

Suitable for secondary entrances, wet rooms, and openings where a washable, corrosion-free surface without coating renewal is required.</td>

Manufactured to order as a complete unit including frame, seals, drive, and control, set and levelled in the prepared opening and commissioned with its operating and safety functions. Suitable where the opening has to combine access with a specific separating duty; the required performance, operating mode, and maintenance regime must be stated per case.</td>
