# Outdoor floor surfaces to outdoor room name mapping

Source: [`outdoor-floor-surfaces-to-outdoor-room-name.mapping.ttl`](sources/mapping-outdoor-floor-surfaces-to-outdoor-room-name.ttl)

External target labels are not shipped in this repository; only codes and IRIs are shown.

## Dataset

- **description (de):** Kompatibilitaetsverknuepfungen von Aussenbodenbelagsprodukten (AOF) zu Aussenraumnamen (RN-OUT-*). skos:relatedMatch: ein Belagsprodukt nennt typische Aussenraumtypen, in denen es ueblich ist. Raumnutzung bleibt ueber Raumnamen, Belag ueber AOF. Nicht bijektiv — Disambiguierung in PBS-Regeln. Begruente Gruendach-Raeume (RN-OUT-GRN) sind hier nicht verknuepft; siehe Dachbekleidungsprodukt-Mapping.
- **description (en):** Compatibility links from outdoor floor surface products (AOF) to outdoor room names (RN-OUT-*). Uses skos:relatedMatch: a surface product suggests typical outdoor space types where it is commonly applied. Space use remains assigned via room name; surface finish remains assigned via AOF. Not bijective — PBS rules disambiguate. Vegetated green-roof spaces (RN-OUT-GRN) are not linked here; see roof covering products mapping.
- **title (de):** Mapping Aussenbodenbeläge zu Aussenraumnamen
- **title (en):** Outdoor floor surfaces to outdoor room name mapping
