<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="label">Gipskarton-Decke</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="definition">Gipskarton-Deckenbekleidung oder abgehaengte GK-Decke.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="description">Geschlossene, fugenlos wirkende Deckenoberfläche aus Gipsplatten, direkt an der Decke befestigt oder davor abgehängt. <td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="description">Closed, jointless-looking ceiling surface from gypsum boards, either fixed directly to the slab or suspended in front of it. Suitable for offices, dwellings, and rooms with fire protection or sound insulation requirements, where the ceiling void carries services and no regular access is needed.</td>
