# Abstract wall covering products

Source: [`wall-covering-products.skos.ttl`](sources/wall-covering-product.ttl)

## Scheme

- **definition (de):** Produkttyp-Klassifikation fuer innere Wandbelaege und Verkleidungen. Fuer Katalog-, Spezifikations- und Kostenworkflows; mit abstrakter Materialklassifikation fuer dominante Substanz kombinieren.
- **definition (en):** Product-type classification for interior wall coverings and claddings. For catalog, specification, and cost workflows; pair with abstract material classification for dominant substance.
- **prefLabel (de):** Abstrakte Wandbelagsprodukte
- **prefLabel (en):** Abstract wall covering products
- **title (en):** Abstract wall covering products

<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="definition">Aufgetragener Innenanstrich oder Beschichtung auf Wandflaechen.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="de" data-field="description">Dünnschichtige, sichtbare Innenwandoberfläche aus Anstrich oder Beschichtung auf Verputz-, Platten- oder Betonuntergrund. <td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="definition">Applied interior paint or coating finish on wall surfaces.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="description">Thin-layer visible interior wall finish from paint or coating on plaster, board, or concrete substrates. Applied wet by brush, roller, or spray in primer and finish coats, including substrate preparation, filling, and sanding to the agreed surface quality. Suitable as a standard finish in all room types, with degree of gloss, wet abrasion resistance, and emission requirements to be specified per room use.</td>
<td class="pbs-lang-col" data-lang="en" data-field="scope_note">Often costed under BKP 285 (innere Oberflaechenbehandlungen) rather than 282; see BKP mapping bridge.</td>
